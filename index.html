Practice go-live kit

Getting a dental office live on an AI billing assistant means turning its payers, fee schedules, and the office manager's write-off policy into posting rules, then watching the first eight weeks closely. This kit does both for a made-up five-office group: Claude reads each policy, checks say which office can go live and who fixes what, and a weekly board flags accounts that drift.

        Data

        The offices, staff, fees, shares, policies, and payment lines are made up and describe no real practice. Payer names and CDT procedure codes are public. The eight weeks are a simulation with the events listed under What it checks; all five offices start together to keep it simple, where the plan brings them on in waves. Deductibles are not modelled. Policy readings and owner notes are real Claude output, cached in the repo.

        Run it yourself

        Python 3.10 or later, standard library only. From the repo folder:

        python3 -m kit.report as_received
python3 -m kit.report after_fixes
python3 -m eval.run_eval
python3 -m unittest
